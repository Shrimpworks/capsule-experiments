## Decision to test first after reconciliation

C5b16 should establish a phase-aware timing/fault baseline around the existing benign
C/Go integration: explain setup refusals and account for durable publication before
teardown. Current source starts cleanup clock after its durable gate returns; the
one-second cleanup clock alone cannot establish a bound from initial action when
publication stalls. Probe this before promoting launch composition or widening any
limit. The next probe must retain exact fixture identities, finite child/harness
containment, no retries, conservative unresolved outcomes, and no guest execution.
It may identify an architecture question; it must not silently weaken durable gates
or invent permission to signal a process without native custody.
