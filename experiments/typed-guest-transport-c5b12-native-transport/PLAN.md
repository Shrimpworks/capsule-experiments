The fixed ABI is copied byte-for-byte from archive merge
`f206e4ef2cd326ee74e5b7b2739c62efe6da7d6d`. Seven providers retain its names;
they do not replace the attempt/profile with a newly invented registration.
The complete driver deliberately cannot link. Only the future Supervisor spawn
owner may transfer the private pipe peers and close its parent copies. Tests
access private state in their own translation unit; no exported setup/reset or
caller-selected descriptor API exists. Serialized invocation and one attempt per
process are explicit construction assumptions, not installed enforcement claims.

Transport uses a stricter local construction deadline: 1,000 ms from endpoint
creation, including readiness/setup. Integration must reconcile this with the
approved runtime deadline before claiming an executable profile. Completion is
checked against the exact 259-byte retained known answer, including header,
payload, digest and final trailer. This is not a general JSON/frame parser.
Completion bytes never establish terminal state, absence, root removal or durable
success. Those facts remain in the other owners and C5b11 recovery oracle.

## Dependency-policy record

- Capability/row: P0-3 fixed source/input/completion envelopes, BUILD-NARROWLY;
  OS descriptor APIs, ADOPT-PLATFORM; native quality checks, TEST-ONLY.
- Candidate: installed Darwin libSystem pipe/fcntl/poll/read/write/close,
  clock_gettime and pthread; Apple clang C17. Exact host/compiler/SDK identifiers
  recorded with results. No new package, crypto, parser, database, VMM or framework.
- Primary source consulted 2026-09-05: Apple XNU public `bsd/sys/fcntl.h`
  (<https://github.com/apple/darwin-xnu/blob/main/bsd/sys/fcntl.h>), including
  per-descriptor F_SETNOSIGPIPE. Installed SDK headers are construction inputs.
- Trust: experiment-only native transport code; authority limited to anonymous
  pipes and one bounded local drain thread. No filesystem path, key, network,
  spawn, guest/backend, or update authority. Apple supplies OS primitives and
  their licenses; Capsule owns new source. No third-party source copied.
- Provenance: immutable predecessor input SHA-256 inventory and generated byte
  arrays checked; compiler/object/import identities retained. No signature,
  independent-builder or minimum-OS claim. Runtime graph is system libSystem;
  full OS implementation graph is not independently audited here.
- Caps: one attempt, six anonymous pipe pairs, one drain thread, 262,369 retained
  completion bytes, 4 KiB scratch, stderr discarded continuously, 1,000 ms total
  local transport budget. No unbounded allocation, queue, frame or retry.
- Faults: failed operation poisons transport; partial I/O never yields facts;
  extra completion remains invalid after further drain. No automatic retry/reset.
- Reproduction: offline installed compiler plus checked-in bytes; two unlinked
  object builds, native tests and ASan/UBSan, deliberate fault/mutation controls.
- Owner: Capsule maintainer; rerun exact corpus on SDK/OS/compiler changes,
  review Apple security updates before any later admission. Removal deletes this
  unwired experiment; no product rollback. Production admission is deferred.
