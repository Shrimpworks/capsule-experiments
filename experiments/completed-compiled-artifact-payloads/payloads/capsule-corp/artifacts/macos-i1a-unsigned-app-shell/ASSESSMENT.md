# I1A assessment

## Decision

**PASSED — unsigned bytes and layout only.** One visible Swift/AppKit status app, two inert
role-private agent placeholders, two exact R2 launcher bundles, and their two exact parser children
rebuild into the I0 tree byte-for-byte across two clean local source/build directories. Closed
metadata and an external manifest digest support independent readback and missing, mixed, extra,
manifest, mode, and substitution refusals.

## What remains blocked

Developer-signed installed I1 remains **BLOCKED**. I1A used no Apple identity, provisioning
profile, Keychain, service registration, application installation, process launch, network,
bootstrap authority, operational key, runtime, backend, or guest. Team `3DDR84M4JS` is retained as
inactive intended metadata only. The Swift linker emitted only its ordinary identity-free ad-hoc
CodeDirectory; that is not Apple enrollment or I1 signing evidence.

Credentialed I1B and Source Validator R3 require a new exact authorization after the final role
profiles, selected Apple Development identity, effective entitlements, constraint bytes, and
supported private-XPC/SMAppService placement inputs exist. Nothing in this result authorizes those
mutations.
